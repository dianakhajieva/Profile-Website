01 / Retail

DVD Rental Analysis

SQLPostgreSQLEDA
Problem

A DVD rental business's raw relational database held the answers to which films and customer segments actually drove revenue — but only if someone was willing to query for them.

Approach

Wrote SQL directly against the PostgreSQL schema to examine customer behavior, rental demand, and film popularity, joining across customers, rentals, and inventory tables to trace revenue back to its source.

Result

A clear, query-backed picture of what was actually driving rental revenue — the foundation for any pricing or inventory decision that followed.

02 / Banking

Banking System Dashboard

SQLPower BIDAX
Problem

Branch and regional performance data for a bank's international transfers business lived in scattered tables — leadership had no single view of revenue, profit, or which branches were actually performing.

Approach

Modeled the transaction data and built a Power BI dashboard with a live regional profit map across Uzbekistan's provinces, a monthly profit trend line, and a branch-by-branch ranking view — designed so leadership could filter by branch, employee, or workload without touching a query.

Result

A single dashboard surfacing $253M in revenue and $5M profit across 50K transactions, with regional and monthly breakdowns that make it obvious where performance is climbing or slipping.

03 / Education

Student Performance Dashboard

Power BIExcelData Modeling
Problem

Academic staff needed a way to see where students across departments and semesters were struggling — not just final grades, but the trend behind them.

Approach

Cleaned and modeled records for nearly 1,800 students across 20 courses and 12 semesters, then built an interactive dashboard breaking down average GPA by semester, department, and enrollment year, plus a gender-split view for equity reporting.

Result

A self-serve dashboard (average GPA 3.17) that lets staff filter straight to the department or semester that needs attention, instead of requesting a custom report each time.
